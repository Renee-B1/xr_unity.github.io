Description of the space you built:

I built a very simple scene with some trees and a castle during dusk.

Describe what your objects are and how you built them (e.g., "I made a table out of 4 cubes and a scaled cube for the tabletop"):

First thing I built was actually a table, then a soccer goal with a net that had collision properties, but my original idea was this wooded area featuring a castle. The main tower is made with a cylinder, cone for the roof, and stairs leading up it, then the smaller tower closest to the camera is made of 4 arches shaped into pointed ellipses then pivoted, two cones for the roof and base, and a torus in the roof for color. And the final tower in the back has an arch flipped to make a bridge leading to it, a cone as the base, a sphere as the domed roof, 2 more elongated arches for the top of the roof, and a cylinder as the walls. The trees could be made directly from the 3D menu which allowed me to do a number of things, like increase its surrounding radius then choose what number of trees that could be created within that vicinity. It also allowed me to change the thickness of the trunks, how many branches I wanted, what pattern I wanted it to follow, and even generate random seeds so each one was unique. This all applied to the branches too.

Any other things you learned or found interesting while working with Unity:

I learned pretty quickly that Unity was not as flexible with creating 3D objects like another software called Blender because it is a game engine first. After playing around with the first few objects (not seen here) and through a lot of google searches, I saw that you could make trees directly from the objects offered and there was something called "ProBuilder". ProBuilder expands on some menus and things you can create which is how I built the entire castle and its floating parts.
