A description of the personal modification you made beyond the tutorial:

The personal modification I made was the a new pick up item which is worth double the points of the original items atop the ramp.

Three key takeaways, in your own words:

1. The easiest part was making each item like the obstacles, walls, floor, and the interactive pieces. Thanks to the tutorial, adding components was really easy and my favorite part of it was probably creating an area for the enemy to move in. However, the hardest part was probably getting the scripting down. I would have been completely lost without the step by step of what each part of the script was doing. I wanted jumping to be my personal addition but the jumping gods told me no which is why I settled on something I knew I could easily recreate.

2. One problem I was having (which is either a good or bad problem to have depending on who you are) was what I wanted my playing field to look like. I know -- in the video it is very generic and a seemingly safe approach to finishing the homework. But with the amount of components you can add, shapes you can create, materials that are modifiable, and lessons on interactivity from the tutorial, I couldn't help but want to do possibly too much.

3. A topic that I learned about was Prefabs and what it does which was pretty cool. We would want to be as efficient as possible when it comes to making assets, especially those that are an exact copy and/ or exhibit the same characteristics as another object. Glad I learned about it so soon, I thought I would burn a hole in my ctrl-c and ctrl-v keys.

Connect this HW to the larger XR documentary project: How do you think these skills will help you with creating an interactive XR documentary?

These skills learned in Unity will help tremendously in creating an interactive documentary. Being able to control exact what goes where, how something looks, what information should be conveyed and in what way, what role the user will play in stepping through the story, and so much more, is what will bring life to this project. Creating a static environment itself whether 2D or 3D, with VR googles, by looking at a screen, reading a book, or listening to music, it is enough to invite the user to step into a world that is beyond their own. But now with scripting, triggering events, playing with transformative properties, anchoring camera angles, being the force behind the time of day or weather, and the impact of the story of a community, it brings to life not just a project, but a true personal experience.
